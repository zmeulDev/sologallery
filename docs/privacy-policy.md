# SoloGallery — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 19 July 2026
**Applies to:** SoloGallery for Android and iOS (`com.zmeul.sologallery`)

## The short version

SoloGallery collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything the
app does happens on your device. There is no account to create, no analytics, no
advertising, and no third party receiving your data — because the app makes no
network requests at all.

## What the app accesses

**Your photo library.** SoloGallery asks for permission to read your photos and
videos so it can show and organize them. It reads them; it does not copy,
upload, or duplicate them. The app stores references to your photos by their
system id, never file copies.

You can grant full access or, on both platforms, limited access to only the
photos you pick. The app works either way.

**Deleting a photo** goes through the operating system's own confirmation, and
removes it from your device library — SoloGallery has nowhere else to remove it
from.

## What the app creates, and where it stays

To make your library searchable, SoloGallery can analyze photos **on your
device** and store what it finds in a private database inside the app's own
storage:

- **Content search** — labels describing what is in a photo, and text found in
  a photo.
- **Face detection** — face locations and numeric face signatures, used to group
  photos of the same person.
- **Memories** — automatically curated collections built from the above.

**All three are off until you turn them on.** SoloGallery asks during setup and
you can change any of them at any time in Settings. Turning one off **deletes**
the data it produced. If you turn all three off, no analysis runs at all.

This analysis uses machine-learning models bundled inside the app. No photo, and
nothing derived from a photo, is ever sent anywhere.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you explicitly share it. When you use the share action,
your device's own share sheet hands the original file to whichever app you
choose — that app's privacy policy then applies, not this one.

## Backups

SoloGallery's database is excluded from automatic cloud backup on Android
(`android:allowBackup="false"`) so the index it builds from your photos is not
copied to any backup service.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Data retention and deletion

All app data lives in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling
SoloGallery removes it entirely. You can also clear derived data at any time by
turning a feature off in Settings, or by using "Rebuild index".

## Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to
disclose, export, correct, or delete on your behalf — you already hold all of
it, on your own device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. A change
that broadens what the app analyzes will also re-ask for your consent in the
app.
